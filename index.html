About

What is Droplab?

Droplab Studio is a digital lab for e‑commerce brands. We design and build custom Shopify stores, then stay on as your growth partner, continually improving your store to increase conversion and results.

  1. 01Custom Shopify themes

    Bespoke themes built from the ground up, fast and true to your brand.

  2. 02Store redesigns

    Already on Shopify? We elevate the design, performance, and user experience of your existing store.

  3. 03UX & conversion

    Every decision made with user experience and conversion in mind.

  4. 04Mobile & desktop optimization

    Stores that look and perform their best on every screen, from phone to desktop.

  5. 05Performance optimization

    Lightning-fast stores that keep customers moving toward checkout.

FAQ

Q.01How does a partnership work?

Partnerships are built around 90 days focused on increasing your website's conversion and results. Throughout that time we keep improving your store, testing and refining what works so the results build on each other.

Q.02How much does a Shopify website cost?

Projects vary depending on scope, features, and the level of customization required. Each store is tailored to the brand, so pricing reflects the complexity of the build, design requirements, and performance goals. After a quick discovery, we provide a clear, fixed quote with no surprises.

Q.03How long does the process take?

Most projects are completed within a few weeks, depending on the size of the store and how quickly content and feedback are provided. We follow a structured process to keep everything moving efficiently while maintaining a high standard of quality.

Q.04What’s included in your Shopify design service?

Everything needed to launch a high-performing Shopify store, including design, development, mobile optimization, and overall user experience. We focus on creating a site that not only looks strong visually but is also built to convert.

Q.05Do you work with existing stores or only new builds?

Both. Whether you're starting from scratch or looking to improve an existing site, we can step in and help elevate the design, performance, and overall user experience.

Q.06What makes you different from other Shopify designers?

We build stores designed to perform while still looking good. Every decision is made with user experience and conversion in mind, so the final result supports your brand while driving measurable results.
